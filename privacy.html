Privacy Policy

TPL QBO Receipt Importer
Draft prepared October 5, 2026; review before publication.

1. Scope and current status

This policy describes the intended data handling of a private, operator-controlled bookkeeping integration. The production connector is not yet deployed. Before production use, the operator must confirm that the implementation follows this policy and update it to describe any additional services or data flows.

2. Information used

For expressly authorized companies, the integration may process company names and identifiers, vendors, account and class lists, transaction dates and amounts, receipt PDFs and their contents, attachment metadata, and records needed for matching or deduplication. It also needs OAuth credentials and connection tokens. The intended integration does not collect Intuit passwords or payment-card credentials. Receipts may themselves contain personal or payment information; unnecessary sensitive details should be removed before processing.

3. Purposes

Data is used for authorized bookkeeping: checking company connections, identifying existing transactions, suggesting classifications, avoiding duplicate uploads, uploading supporting PDFs, and linking documents to records. Separate companies' connections and records must remain identified separately.

4. Storage and services

The planned connector runs on the operator's Windows computer. Local files, logs, and backups may retain financial information. Documents in a OneDrive-synchronized folder may also be stored by Microsoft. Uploaded PDFs and related accounting records are sent to Intuit and retained in QuickBooks Online under that service's terms.

Hermes uses configured model and service providers. When receipt contents or accounting information are included in an AI request, that information may be transmitted to the selected provider; it is not necessarily processed only on the local computer. Provider logging, retention, training practices, and processing locations depend on the selected service and account settings. The operator must review those terms and approve the data flow before sending financial information. This policy does not promise that third-party providers never retain data or use it for training.

GitHub Pages hosts only the public policy documents, not the connector's financial records or credentials. GitHub may process visitor connection information according to its own privacy statement. These policy pages contain no application-added analytics, tracking scripts, or advertising.

5. Disclosure

The integration is not intended to sell financial or personal information or use it for advertising. Data is disclosed only as needed to authorized users, configured service providers supporting the approved workflow, or when required by law. Any new provider or materially different use must be reviewed before it is enabled.

6. Safeguards

Production use requires protected credential storage, restricted access to the computer and financial files, and checks that operations target the intended company. Secrets must not be committed to GitHub, included in public pages, or exposed in routine logs. Security measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention and deletion

The operator controls local document and bookkeeping-record retention, including retention needed for tax records. There is no automatic deletion schedule currently implemented. Authorized users may contact the administrator to request access, correction, or deletion, subject to authority to act and applicable recordkeeping requirements. Copies in QuickBooks, OneDrive, backups, or other providers may need separate deletion and may be subject to those providers' retention rules.

8. Disconnecting

Authorization may be revoked through QuickBooks Online. The operator should also remove stored connection tokens. Disconnecting does not automatically delete previously uploaded attachments, transactions, local documents, or backups.

9. Contact and updates

For privacy questions or requests, contact the integration administrator at tplpropertiesllc@gmail.com. Update this policy when the integration's actual data handling changes.
